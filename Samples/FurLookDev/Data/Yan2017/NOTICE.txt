Yan et al. (2017) Fur Reflectance Fitted Dataset
================================================

Source:
  Ling-Qi Yan, Henrik Wann Jensen, and Ravi Ramamoorthi,
  "An Efficient and Practical Near and Far Field Fur Reflectance Model",
  ACM Transactions on Graphics (Proceedings of SIGGRAPH 2017), 36(4).
  Project page: https://sites.cs.ucsb.edu/~lingqi/project_page/fur2/index.html

Included fitted reflectance tables (phi in [70..290] -> [-20..200] deg,
theta in [40..80] -> [50..10] deg, gantry coordinates):
  - HumanFitted.txt   (negligible medulla reference, kappa ~ 0)
  - CatFitted.txt     (moderate medulla animal fur)
  - RabbitFitted.txt  (prominent turbid medulla fur)
  - RaccoonFitted.txt (high medulla / banded guard fur)
  - DeerFitted.txt    (wide-angle air-filled medulla fur)

Included strictly for non-commercial validation and visual/numerical
comparison inside VividRP's FurLookDev benchmark scene.
